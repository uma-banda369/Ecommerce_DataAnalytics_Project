**Column	Meaning	Data Type**	
order_id:	Unique order number	Text	
order_date:	Date of order	Date	
customer_id:	Unique customer identifier	Text	
product_category:	Product category	Text/Categorical	
region:	Customer/order region	Text/Categorical	
quantity:	Number of products purchased	Integer	
unit_price:	Price of one item	Decimal/Numeric	
discount:	Discount offered on product	Decimal/Percentage	
payment_method:	Method used by the customer to pay for the order	Text/Categorical	
delivery_days:	Number of days taken to deliver the order	Integer	
customer_rating:	Rating given by the customer after receiving the order	Integer/Decimal	
revenue:	Total revenue generated from the order after applying the discount	Decimal/Numeric	Revenue = Quantity × Unit Price × (1 − Discount)
