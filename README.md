# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and customer transaction data to identify revenue drivers, customer behavior, product performance, profitability trends, and opportunities for business growth.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to SQL analysis, data modeling, Power BI visualization, and business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to help an e-commerce business:

* Monitor revenue and profitability
* Identify high-performing products and categories
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Analyze regional sales performance
* Identify repeat and high-value customers
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability
* Identify opportunities for revenue and customer growth

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions

1. What are the company's overall revenue, profit, and order metrics?
2. How does revenue change over time?
3. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
4. Which products contribute the most to revenue and profit?
5. Which regions generate the highest sales?
6. Who are the highest-value customers?
7. What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
8. How does discounting affect profitability?
9. Which categories have high revenue but low profit margins?
10. What business actions can improve revenue and profitability?
